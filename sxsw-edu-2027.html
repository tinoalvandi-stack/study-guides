What's a track? SXSW EDU sorts its hundreds of sessions into themed groups it calls tracks, the way a textbook has units. T Our four groups each follow one SXSW EDU track for the weekend and a second one Monday and Tuesday, so the class hears eight themes instead of one. The eight were chosen so every kind of student has two: tech, health and sports, arts, leadership, academics, business, careers, and the wider world. Session picks get made when the 2027 schedule posts.

The interview task

Three conversations.
One shared field card.

Each student logs a reserved mentor session, a track presenter conversation, and a floor conversation with an exhibitor, competition finalist, or out-of-state educator. Bertram et al., 2017 Atkins et al., 2020

Field card

Questions 1–3 are the same for everyone. Each group writes 4 and 5 for both of its tracks in prep session 2. Presenter conversations use two of the five.

    Consent: "We're high school students from Las Vegas doing a class project. Okay if we take notes and quote you?"
  2. 1
    What problem were you solving, and what failed first?
  3. 2

FAQ for parents

Quick answers.

Is this trip approved, and what will it cost?

Not yet. This is the team's proposal. Costs fill in once we have quotes. Cost worksheet.

How much school will students miss?

None. March 13–14 is a weekend and March 15–16 falls in Gorman's spring break. Gorman calendar

Will students move around Austin on their own?

No. Each group of seven stays with its chaperone; the whole group meets for keynotes, the competition, meals, and the regroup. Venues are within about five blocks of each other.

Does the badge cover every SXSW event?

No. An EDU badge covers SXSW EDU plus selected Crossover sessions on March 15–16. E

Which time zone is the schedule in?

Austin times are Central, two hours ahead of Las Vegas. Flight times are shown in local time at each airport.

What comes home?

Four ten-minute presentations, four one-page briefs, a "who we met" list, and each student's future-self paragraph. Report-back plan.
