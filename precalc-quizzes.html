PCBC H Vectors & Dot Product: Memorize Sheet
everything below is from memory on quiz day

Component form

PQ = ⟨x₂ − x₁, y₂ − y₁⟩ (terminal − initial)
⟨a, b⟩ = ai + bj, i = ⟨1, 0⟩, j = ⟨0, 1⟩
|⟨a, b⟩| = √(a² + b²)
unit vector: magnitude 1

Dot product

u · v = ac + bd (a number, not a vector)
u · u = |u|²
u · v = v · u
u · (v + w) = u · v + u · w
(ku) · v = k(u · v) = u · (kv)

Magnitude and direction

a = |v|cos θ (horizontal)
b = |v|sin θ (vertical)
v = ⟨|v|cos θ, |v|sin θ⟩
tan θ = b/a, a ≠ 0 (reference angle)
report θ in [0, 2π)

Angle between

cos θ = (u · v)/(|u||v|), 0° ≤ θ ≤ 180°
orthogonal ⟺ u · v = 0
dot > 0: acute
dot = 0: right
dot < 0: obtuse

Quadrant fix

tan⁻¹ only answers in QI or QIV. signs of ⟨a, b⟩ give the quadrant:
QI (+, +): θ = ref
QII (−, +): θ = 180° − ref
QIII (−, −): θ = 180° + ref
QIV (+, −): θ = 360° − ref

Two forces at a point

angle θ between them ⇒ use the supplement 180° − θ in the triangle
x = √(a² + b² − 2ab cos C)
one radical, round at the very end

Operations

⟨a, b⟩ ± ⟨c, d⟩ = ⟨a ± c, b ± d⟩
k⟨a, b⟩ = ⟨ka, kb⟩
resultant = the sum: tip to tail, or the diagonal of the parallelogram
addition commutes, subtraction does not

Exact values

θsin θcos θ

signs by quadrant (ASTC): QI all +; QII sin +; QIII tan +; QIV cos +

Find 2u + v − 6v and |u − v| for the given vectors u and v. Write 2u + v − 6v in component form AND in terms of i and j. (4 points)

1.u = ⟨−3, 4⟩, v = ⟨2, −6⟩
combine the like vectors first: v − 6v = −5v, so the expression is 2u − 5v
2u = ⟨−6, 8⟩, −5v = ⟨−10, 30⟩
2u + v − 6v = ⟨−6 + (−10), 8 + 30⟩ = ⟨−16, 38⟩ = −16i + 38j
u − v = ⟨−5, 10⟩ ⇒ |u − v| = √(25 + 100) = √125 = 5√5

Express the vector with initial point P and terminal point Q in component form AND in terms of i and j. (2 points)

2.P(−3, 5), Q(6, −7)
terminal minus initial: PQ = ⟨6 − (−3), −7 − 5⟩
PQ = ⟨9, −12⟩ = 9i − 12j

Find the horizontal and vertical components of each vector. Then write u + v in component form AND in terms of i and j, and find |u + v|. (5 points)

3.|u| = 6, θ = 150°; |v| = 4, θ = 30°
u = ⟨6cos 150°, 6sin 150°⟩: QII, reference angle 30°, so cos = −√3/2, sin = 1/2
u = ⟨−3√3, 3⟩ (horizontal −3√3, vertical 3)
v = ⟨4cos 30°, 4sin 30°⟩ = ⟨2√3, 2⟩
u + v = ⟨−3√3 + 2√3, 3 + 2⟩ = ⟨−√3, 5⟩ = −√3 i + 5j
|u + v| = √(3 + 25) = √28 = 2√7

Find the magnitude and direction of the vector. Write your answer in radians. (3 points)

4.u = ⟨−6, −6√3⟩
|u| = √((−6)² + (−6√3)²) = √(36 + 108) = √144 = 12
tanθ = b/a = −6√3/−6 = √3 ⇒ reference angle = π/3
a < 0 and b < 0 ⇒ QIII, so θ = π + π/3 = 4π/3

Find the dot product of the given vectors. Then determine if u and v are orthogonal. (4 points)

5.u = ⟨4, −2⟩, v = ⟨−3, 5⟩. Find (−2u)·v and u·(u + v).
−2u = ⟨−8, 4⟩ ⇒ (−2u)·v = (−8)(−3) + (4)(5) = 24 + 20 = 44
check: u·v = (4)(−3) + (−2)(5) = −22, and (ku)·v = k(u·v) = (−2)(−22) = 44 ✓
u + v = ⟨1, 3⟩ ⇒ u·(u + v) = (4)(1) + (−2)(3) = −2
u·v = −22 ≠ 0, so not orthogonal (negative ⇒ obtuse angle)

Find the angle between the given vectors. Round to the nearest tenth of a degree. (3 points)

6.u = ⟨5, 2⟩, v = ⟨−1, 4⟩
u·v = (5)(−1) + (2)(4) = 3
|u| = √29, |v| = √17
cosθ = u·v/|u||v| = 3/√29√17 = 3/√493 ≈ 0.1351
θ = cos⁻¹(0.1351) ≈ 82.2° (positive dot product ⇒ acute ✓)

Two forces act at a point in the plane. The angle between the two forces is given. Find the magnitude of the resultant force. Round to the nearest tenth. (4 points)

7.forces of 180 lb and 320 lb, forming an angle of 72°
the resultant is the diagonal of the parallelogram; the angle across from it is 180° − 72° = 108°
x = √(180² + 320² − 2(180)(320)cos 108°)
x ≈ 412.8 lb (larger than either force, which fits a 72° spread)
