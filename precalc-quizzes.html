PCBC H Vectors & Dot Product: Memorize Sheet
everything below is from memory on quiz day

Component form

PQ = ⟨x₂ − x₁, y₂ − y₁⟩ (terminal − initial)
⟨a, b⟩ = ai + bj, i = ⟨1, 0⟩, j = ⟨0, 1⟩
|⟨a, b⟩| = √(a² + b²)
unit vector: magnitude 1

Dot product

u · v = ac + bd (a number, not a vector)
u · u = |u|²
u · v = v · u
u · (v + w) = u · v + u · w
(ku) · v = k(u · v) = u · (kv)

Magnitude and direction

a = |v|cos θ (horizontal)
b = |v|sin θ (vertical)
v = ⟨|v|cos θ, |v|sin θ⟩
tan θ = b/a, a ≠ 0 (reference angle)
report θ in [0, 2π)

Angle between

cos θ = (u · v)/(|u||v|), 0° ≤ θ ≤ 180°
orthogonal ⟺ u · v = 0
dot > 0: acute
dot = 0: right
dot < 0: obtuse

Quadrant fix

tan⁻¹ only answers in QI or QIV. signs of ⟨a, b⟩ give the quadrant:
QI (+, +): θ = ref
QII (−, +): θ = 180° − ref
QIII (−, −): θ = 180° + ref
QIV (+, −): θ = 360° − ref

Two forces at a point

angle θ between them ⇒ use the supplement 180° − θ in the triangle
x = √(a² + b² − 2ab cos C)
one radical, round at the very end

Operations

⟨a, b⟩ ± ⟨c, d⟩ = ⟨a ± c, b ± d⟩
k⟨a, b⟩ = ⟨ka, kb⟩
|kv| = |k||v| (the sign never shortens it)
resultant = the sum: tip to tail, or the diagonal of the parallelogram
addition commutes, subtraction does not

Exact values

Find 3u + v − 5v and |2u − v| for the given vectors u and v. Write 3u + v − 5v in component form AND in terms of i and j. (3 points)

1.u = −3i + 2j, v = 4i − j
collect first: v − 5v = −4v, so the expression is 3u − 4v
u = ⟨−3, 2⟩, v = ⟨4, −1⟩; 3u = ⟨−9, 6⟩, 4v = ⟨16, −4⟩
3u + v − 5v = ⟨−9 − 16, 6 − (−4)⟩ = ⟨−25, 10⟩ = −25i + 10j
2u − v = ⟨−6 − 4, 4 − (−1)⟩ = ⟨−10, 5⟩
|2u − v| = √(100 + 25) = √125 = 5√5

Express the vector with initial point P and terminal point Q in component form AND in terms of i and j. Then find |−2PQ|. (2 points)

2.P(6, −2), Q(−2, 4)
PQ = ⟨−2 − 6, 4 − (−2)⟩ = ⟨−8, 6⟩ = −8i + 6j
|PQ| = √(64 + 36) = 10
scaling multiplies the length by |k|: |−2PQ| = 2(10) = 20 (the sign does not shorten it)

Find the horizontal and vertical components of each vector. Then write u + v in component form AND in terms of i and j, and find |u + v|. (3 points)

3.|u| = 8, θ = π; |v| = 4, θ = π/3
u = ⟨8cos π, 8sin π⟩ = ⟨8(−1), 8(0)⟩ = ⟨−8, 0⟩ (horizontal −8, vertical 0)
v = ⟨4cos(π/3), 4sin(π/3)⟩ = ⟨2, 2√3⟩
u + v = ⟨−8 + 2, 0 + 2√3⟩ = ⟨−6, 2√3⟩ = −6i + 2√3 j
|u + v| = √(36 + 12) = √48 = 4√3

Find the magnitude and direction of the vector. Write your answer in radians. (3 points)

4.u = −(√3/2)i + (1/2)j
|u| = √(3/4 + 1/4) = √1 = 1 (a unit vector, the fractions are not a mistake)
tan θ = (1/2)/(−√3/2) = −1/√3 ⇒ reference angle = π/6
a < 0, b > 0 ⇒ QII, so θ = π − π/6 = 5π/6

Find (2u)·v and u·(u + v) for the given vectors. Then determine if u and v are orthogonal and state whether the angle between them is acute, right, or obtuse. (3 points)

5.u = 4i, v = −3i + 5j
the missing component is a zero: u = ⟨4, 0⟩, v = ⟨−3, 5⟩
u·v = (4)(−3) + (0)(5) = −12
(2u)·v = 2(u·v) = −24
u + v = ⟨1, 5⟩ ⇒ u·(u + v) = (4)(1) + (0)(5) = 4
u·v = −12 ≠ 0, so not orthogonal; negative ⇒ obtuse

Find the angle between the given vectors. Round to the nearest tenth of a degree. (3 points)

6.u = 3i + 4√2 j, v = √2 i − j

Two forces act at a point in the plane. The angle between the two forces is given. Find the magnitude of the resultant force. Round to the nearest tenth. (3 points)

7.forces of 240 N and 175 N, forming an angle of 63°
the resultant is the diagonal; the angle across from it is 180° − 63° = 117°
x = √(240² + 175² − 2(240)(175)cos 117°)
x ≈ 355.5 N
